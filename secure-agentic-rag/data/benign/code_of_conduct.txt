Code of Conduct

Our company is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment for all employees, contractors, and visitors.

1. Respect and Inclusion
Treat everyone with dignity and respect. Harassment, discrimination, and bullying are strictly prohibited and will result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

2. Professional Behavior
Maintain professional conduct in all business interactions, including online communications. Represent the company positively in public and on social media.

3. Conflicts of Interest
Disclose any potential conflicts of interest to your manager and the ethics committee. Do not engage in business activities that compete with the company.

4. Confidentiality
Protect confidential business information. Do not share internal strategies, financial data, or customer information outside authorized channels.

5. Reporting Violations
If you witness or experience conduct that violates this policy, report it through your manager, HR, or the anonymous ethics hotline. Retaliation against reporters is prohibited.

6. Enforcement
Violations of this code will be investigated promptly. Consequences range from verbal warning to immediate termination depending on severity.
