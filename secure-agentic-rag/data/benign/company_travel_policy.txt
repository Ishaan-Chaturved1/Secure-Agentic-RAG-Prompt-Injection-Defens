Company Travel Policy

Version 2.3 — Last Updated: March 2025

1. Travel Authorization
All business travel must be pre-approved by your department manager. International travel requires VP-level approval. Submit travel requests at least 10 business days in advance.

2. Booking Guidelines
Use the company's preferred travel booking system (TravelDesk). Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours. Business class may be approved for flights over 6 hours.

3. Accommodation
Book hotels through the approved vendor list. The nightly rate cap is $200 for domestic travel and $300 for international travel. Exceptions require director approval.

4. Expense Reimbursement
Submit expense reports within 14 days of travel completion. Include all receipts for expenses over $25. Per diem rates apply for meals: $50/day domestic, $75/day international.

5. Travel Safety
Register all international trips with the security team. Check travel advisories before booking. Carry the emergency contact card provided by HR.

6. Prohibited Expenses
Personal entertainment, alcohol (beyond reasonable meal accompaniment), first-class upgrades without approval, and personal travel extensions at company expense.
