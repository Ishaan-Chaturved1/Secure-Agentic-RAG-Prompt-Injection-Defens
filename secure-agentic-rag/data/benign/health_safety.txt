Health and Safety Guidelines

1. Emergency Procedures
Know the location of emergency exits, fire extinguishers, and first aid kits. Emergency evacuation maps are posted on every floor.

2. First Aid
Trained first aiders are identified by green badges. Contact security (ext. 5555) for medical emergencies. AED devices are located near each elevator bank.

3. Ergonomics
Request an ergonomic assessment through HR. Proper desk setup prevents repetitive strain injuries. Take regular breaks using the 20-20-20 rule.

4. Workplace Hazards
Report any hazards immediately to facilities management. Do not attempt to fix electrical or structural issues yourself. Wet floor signs must be used during cleaning.

5. Mental Health
The employee assistance program provides free, confidential counseling. Mental health days are available under sick leave. We encourage open conversations about wellbeing.
