Employee Leave Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

1. Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at a rate of 1.67 days per month. Unused leave may be carried over up to a maximum of 5 days.

2. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.

3. Personal Leave
Up to 3 days of personal leave are available for personal matters such as moving, family emergencies, or appointments.

4. Parental Leave
Primary caregivers: 16 weeks paid leave. Secondary caregivers: 4 weeks paid leave.

5. How to Request Leave
Submit leave requests through the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for planned leave. Emergency leave should be reported to your direct manager within 24 hours.

6. Leave Approval
All leave requests are subject to manager approval. During peak business periods, leave requests may be prioritized based on team needs.

Contact HR at hr@company-example.com for questions about the leave policy.
