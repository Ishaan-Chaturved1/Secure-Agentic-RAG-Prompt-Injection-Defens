Expense Reimbursement Policy

1. Eligible Expenses
Business meals, transportation, office supplies, professional development, and conference fees are eligible for reimbursement when pre-approved.

2. Submission Process
- Submit expense reports through the finance portal
- Include original receipts or digital copies
- Categorize expenses correctly
- Submit within 30 days of the expense

3. Approval Chain
Expenses under $100: Manager approval
Expenses $100-$500: Director approval
Expenses over $500: VP approval

4. Processing Time
Approved reimbursements are processed within 10 business days and added to the next payroll cycle.

5. Corporate Card
Employees with a corporate card must reconcile transactions monthly. Misuse of the corporate card will result in disciplinary action including potential termination.

6. Non-Reimbursable Expenses
Personal items, fines, parking tickets, personal phone upgrades, and entertainment not directly related to business purposes.
