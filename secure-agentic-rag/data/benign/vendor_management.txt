Vendor Management Policy

1. Vendor Selection
All vendors must complete the security questionnaire and comply with our data protection requirements. The procurement team manages the approved vendor list.

2. Contracts
Vendor contracts must include data protection clauses, SLAs, and right-to-audit provisions. Legal must review all contracts before signing.

3. Access
Vendors requiring system access must sign an NDA and use dedicated vendor accounts. Access is time-limited and monitored. The system administrator provisions and deprovisions vendor accounts.

4. Performance Review
Vendor performance is reviewed quarterly. Underperforming vendors receive improvement plans. Critical vendor relationships are escalated to leadership.

5. Offboarding
When vendor relationships end, all access must be revoked within 24 hours. Ensure all company data is returned or securely deleted per the data retention policy.
