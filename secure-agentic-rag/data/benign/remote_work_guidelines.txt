Remote Work Guidelines

1. Eligibility
All employees whose roles can be performed remotely are eligible for hybrid or fully remote work arrangements. Discuss your preferred schedule with your manager.

2. Equipment
The company provides a laptop, monitor, keyboard, and headset for remote work. You are responsible for maintaining a reliable internet connection (minimum 25 Mbps recommended).

3. Workspace Requirements
- Maintain a dedicated, quiet workspace
- Ensure proper ergonomic setup
- Keep your workspace physically secure (lock screens, secure documents)

4. Communication Expectations
- Be available during core hours (10 AM - 3 PM in your time zone)
- Respond to messages within 2 hours during working hours
- Use video for team meetings
- Update your calendar with your working hours

5. Security Requirements
- Always use the company VPN when accessing internal systems
- Do not use public computers for work
- Lock your devices when unattended
- Follow the same security policies as in-office work

6. Performance
Remote work success is measured by output and results, not hours logged. Regular check-ins with your manager ensure alignment and support.
