HR Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I update my personal information?
A: Log into the HR portal and navigate to "My Profile." You can update your address, phone number, and emergency contacts directly. Name changes require supporting documentation.

Q: When are performance reviews conducted?
A: Annual performance reviews are conducted in November. Mid-year check-ins occur in May. Your manager will schedule a 1-on-1 meeting to discuss your performance and development goals.

Q: How do I report workplace concerns?
A: You can report concerns through your manager, HR, or the anonymous ethics hotline at 1-800-555-ETHICS. All reports are investigated confidentially.

Q: What benefits are available?
A: Full-time employees receive health insurance, dental, vision, 401(k) with company match, life insurance, and an employee assistance program. Part-time employees working 20+ hours receive partial benefits.

Q: How does the promotion process work?
A: Promotions are based on performance, demonstrated competencies, and business need. Discuss career growth with your manager during performance reviews. Senior positions require interview panel approval.

Q: Can I transfer to another department?
A: Internal transfers are encouraged. Post your interest on the internal job board. You must have been in your current role for at least 12 months and have manager support.

Q: What is the dress code?
A: Business casual is standard. Client-facing meetings may require business professional attire. Casual Fridays allow jeans and company-branded apparel.

Q: How do I request workplace accommodations?
A: Contact HR with your accommodation request. We follow ADA guidelines and work with employees to provide reasonable accommodations.
