Professional Development Program

1. Training Budget
Each employee receives a $2,000 annual professional development budget. This covers courses, certifications, conferences, and books.

2. Certification Support
The company reimburses certification exam fees upon passing. Study time of up to 4 hours per week during work hours is permitted with manager approval.

3. Internal Training
Monthly lunch-and-learn sessions cover topics from technical skills to leadership development. Participation is voluntary and encouraged.

4. Mentorship
Join the mentorship program to be paired with a senior colleague. Both mentors and mentees benefit from knowledge sharing.

5. Tuition Assistance
For degree programs relevant to your role, the company reimburses up to $5,000 per year. A minimum of one year of service is required.
