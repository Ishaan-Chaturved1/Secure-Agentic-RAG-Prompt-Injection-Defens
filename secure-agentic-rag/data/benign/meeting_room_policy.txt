Meeting Room Booking Policy

1. Booking System
Reserve meeting rooms through the calendar application. Walk-in use is only permitted if the room is available for the next 30 minutes.

2. Room Sizes
- Huddle rooms (2-4 people): No booking required for under 30 minutes
- Medium rooms (5-10 people): Book at least 2 hours in advance
- Large conference rooms (10-20 people): Book at least 24 hours in advance
- Auditorium (50+ people): Book at least 1 week in advance with facilities approval

3. Etiquette
- Release rooms you no longer need
- Clean up after your meeting
- End on time to respect the next booking
- Keep food and drinks contained

4. Technology
All rooms include video conferencing capability. Test equipment before important meetings. Report technical issues to IT support.

5. Cancellation
Cancel bookings at least 1 hour in advance. Repeated no-shows may result in booking privilege restrictions.
