Data Retention and Deletion Policy

1. Purpose
This policy defines how long business data is retained and when it must be securely deleted to comply with regulatory requirements.

2. Retention Periods
- Customer data: 7 years after account closure
- Financial records: 10 years
- Employee records: 7 years after termination
- System logs: 90 days (security logs: 1 year)
- Email archives: 5 years

3. Secure Deletion
When data reaches end-of-life, it must be securely deleted using approved methods:
- Digital data: Cryptographic erasure or DOD 5220.22-M standard
- Physical media: Shredding through approved vendor
- Database records: Use the approved data lifecycle management tool

4. Legal Hold
If data is subject to litigation or regulatory investigation, normal deletion schedules are suspended. Contact Legal before deleting any data that might be relevant to pending matters.

5. Responsibilities
Data owners are responsible for classifying their data and ensuring proper retention. The IT Security team audits compliance quarterly. System administrators must not delete data outside of the approved process.

6. Exceptions
Requests for early deletion or extended retention must be approved by the Data Governance Committee.
